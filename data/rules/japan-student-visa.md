Source page: "General visa: Student," Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan — listed under Consular Services > VISA > **Work or Long-term stay**. Page dated March 17, 2023 (as listed on the page itself).

**This is a distinct, separate category from short-term visa-exempt entry.** This page sits under MOFA's "Work or Long-term stay" visa grouping, not the "Short-term stay" grouping. It is not the same thing as the Note 8 mechanism described in japan-visa-exemption-list.md, which lets certain visa-exempt nationalities (Austria, Germany, Ireland, Liechtenstein, Mexico, Switzerland, UK) request an extension of an otherwise-visa-free short stay up to 6 months for any purpose via the Regional Immigration Bureau. The formal Student visa documented here is a purpose-built long-term visa category, applied for at a Japanese embassy/consulate before travel, and is not restricted to nationalities with visa-exemption arrangements — it applies regardless of the applicant's nationality (subject to the standard visa-requirement question, which is covered separately in the corpus's per-nationality routing files).

**Period of stay**: "A term of individually designated by the Minister of Justice (4 years and 3 months or less)."

**Necessary documents** (as of the October 11, 2022 document-requirement update referenced on the page):
- Passport.
- One visa application form (nationals of Russia, CIS countries, or Georgia must submit two).
- One photograph (nationals of Russia, CIS countries, or Georgia must submit two).
- **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** — original or one copy. If the applicant has an electronic COE, it may be presented at the visa application counter or a printed copy submitted.
- Chinese nationals must also submit: a copy of the Chinese Family Register; a Temporary Residence Permit or Residence Certificate (if the applicant has no family register in the region under the jurisdiction of the relevant embassy/consulate); a Questionnaire (available from approved agencies or the Japanese embassy/consulate website in China); a graduation certificate; and the employment certificate of the financial supporter.
- Depending on nationality, other documents may be required — applicants should check with the relevant Japanese embassy/consulate.

**What a Certificate of Eligibility is**: Issued by a regional immigration authority under the Immigration Services Agency of Japan, a COE "certifies that the foreign national conforms to the conditions for landing in Japan (which means the activity stated in the application is not false and it is one of the activities (except Temporary Visitor) stipulated in the Immigration Control Act)." A proxy inside Japan (e.g., the school) can apply for the COE on the student's behalf. Presenting a COE lets the visa application and the landing (immigration) examination "be processed smoothly," but the page explicitly cautions: "a COE does not guarantee the issuance of a visa."

**Applying without a COE**: A visa application for the Student category can be made without a COE, but the applicant "will be required to submit a large amount of verification documents and it could take a long time (several months) to process" — versus the standard 5-working-day processing period MOFA states for applications generally (per mofa.go.jp's visa FAQ page, fetched separately this session).

**Fee**: Not stated on this page. See japan-visa-fees.md, which documents MOFA's general visa issuance fee schedule (single-entry, multiple-entry, transit) — that file notes the fee schedule as `confidence: uncertain` because a July 1, 2026 fee revision could not be confirmed against a post-revision official snapshot. No Student-visa-specific fee figure was found on either page during this session.

**Gap/caveat**: This page does not itemize financial-sufficiency requirements, English/Japanese language requirements, or part-time work rules for Student-visa holders — those were not found on this specific page and should not be assumed without further confirmation from Japan's Immigration Services Agency (a different domain from mofa.go.jp, not fetched in this session).
