Source pages: "Student visa: Overview" (https://www.gov.uk/student-visa) and "Student visa: Your course" (https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/course), GOV.UK, fetched directly (live pages).

**Eligibility**: You can apply for a Student visa if you are 16 or older and:
- Have been offered a place on a course by a licensed student sponsor.
- Have enough money to support yourself and pay for your course (specific amount varies by individual circumstances; not itemized on this page).
- Can speak, read, write, and understand English.
- If 16 or 17, have parental consent.

**Qualifying course types**:
- Below degree level: a full-time course leading to a qualification below degree level (RQF level 3, 4, or 5) with at least 15 hours/week of organised daytime study.
- Degree level and above: full-time courses at RQF level 6, 7, or 8.
- Overseas students: full-time degree-level courses delivered as part of a longer overseas program.
- Postgraduate study: part-time courses at RQF level 7 or above.
- English language courses: at B2 level or higher.

**CAS requirement**: Applicants must obtain a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) reference number from their education provider before applying, and must apply for the visa within 6 months of receiving the CAS.

**Application timeline**:
- From outside the UK: earliest application is 6 months before the course start date; typical decision time is 3 weeks.
- From inside the UK: earliest application is 3 months before the course start date; typical decision time is 8 weeks; the new course must start within 28 days of the current visa's expiry.

**Stay duration**: For courses of approximately 6 months, applicants can typically arrive up to 1 week before the course starts and remain for the duration of the course plus any permitted post-study period (exact post-study period not quantified on the fetched pages). For longer courses: if 18+ and the course is at degree level, applicants can usually stay up to 5 years; if below degree level, usually up to 2 years.

**Fees**: Application fee stated on the page: £558. A healthcare (immigration health) surcharge also applies, with the amount based on visa duration (specific surcharge amount not stated on the fetched page).

**Gap/caveat**: The fetched pages did not specify a minimum course-length threshold below which an applicant would need a different visa category (e.g., a Short-term Study visa) instead of the Student route — that distinction was not found in the content retrieved during this session and should not be assumed without further confirmation.
