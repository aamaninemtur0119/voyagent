Source: "Visa policy," European Commission, Migration and Home Affairs (home-affairs.ec.europa.eu), fetched directly (live page).

**China and India are among the countries whose nationals require a Schengen (type C) short-stay visa** to enter the Schengen area. This is confirmed directly on the official EU Migration and Home Affairs visa policy page.

**Legal basis**: The definitive, complete list of third countries whose nationals must hold a visa to cross external Schengen borders is set out in **Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2018/1806** of the European Parliament and of the Council. (Full regulation text: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2020-05/regulation-2018-1806.pdf) This session did not enumerate the complete Annex I country list from the regulation text itself, but the Commission's own policy summary page directly names China and India as visa-required nationalities.

**Note on scope**: A Schengen visa, once issued, is generally valid for travel across every country in the Schengen area (i.e., it is not country-specific once granted), simplifying multi-country travel within the area.

For related rules see: schengen-90-180-rule.md (duration limits and what applies beyond 90 days) and schengen-airport-transit-visa.md (separate airport transit visa nationality rules, which follow a different list and process than the type C short-stay visa).
