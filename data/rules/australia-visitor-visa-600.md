## Visitor visa (subclass 600) — general

**Purpose**: "For tourists, business visitors or to visit family for 3, 6 or 12 months."

**Basic eligibility** (as stated on the page): applicants must
- be a genuine visitor, and
- have enough funds to support their stay and leave.

**Streams** (each with its own dedicated sub-page not independently fetched in this session, so stream-specific fees/criteria are not detailed here to avoid fabrication):
- Tourist stream
- Business Visitor stream
- Sponsored Family stream
- Approved Destination Status stream (for group tours from China arranged via approved travel agents)

**Relationship to eVisitor/ETA**: Per the page's ETA-related help content: "European passport holders may be eligible for an eVisitor (subclass 651)... Australian citizens are ineligible to apply for an ETA." The clear implication (and standard structure of Australia's visa system) is that Visitor visa subclass 600 is the general/fallback visa application route for travelers who are not eligible for either the no-cost eVisitor (subclass 651, restricted to European passport holders) or the ETA (subclass 601, restricted to a specific list of eligible passports — see australia-eta-601.md). Nationalities not on either of those eligible-passport lists — including Brazil and China — must apply for a Visitor visa (subclass 600) to visit Australia.

**Gap/caveat**: This session did not independently fetch the Tourist stream, Business Visitor stream, or Sponsored Family stream sub-pages, so specific fees, processing times, or stream-specific document requirements for subclass 600 are not included here and should not be assumed from this file.

## eVisitor (subclass 651) — CONFIRMED restricted to European passport holders, does NOT include Brazil

Per the "Subclass 651 eVisitor" page (Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2026-07-30 of immi.homeaffairs.gov.au), the eligibility criteria state: "You must be a citizen of and hold a valid passport from one of these countries to be eligible for the eVisitor":

Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, The Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Republic of San Marino, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom – British Citizen, Vatican City (passport must indicate national of the Vatican City / Holy See).

The page explicitly states applicants CANNOT apply with: a non-citizen passport, certificate of identity, or other travel document (including a Titre de Voyage); or a British National Overseas, British Dependent Territories Citizen, British Overseas Citizen, British Protected Person, or British Subject passport.

**Brazil is confirmed NOT on this list.** The eVisitor list is composed entirely of European countries/microstates plus a UK-citizen-specific category and Vatican City — no South American country, including Brazil, appears anywhere in the eligible-passport list.

**eVisitor terms** (from the same source): Cost: **Free**. Stay: up to 3 months at a time. Permitted activities: visit family or friends; cruise or holiday; undertake business visitor activities; study or train for up to 3 months in some circumstances.
