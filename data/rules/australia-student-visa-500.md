Source: "Subclass 500 Student visa," Australian Department of Home Affairs (immi.homeaffairs.gov.au), fetched live in this session (embedded JSON content block).

**Core process** (as stated on the page, under "Process" / "You must"):
- Apply online, in or outside Australia.
- Be enrolled in a course of study in Australia and hold a valid **Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)** when the Department decides the visa.
- Hold **Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)**, or fall into one of the exemption categories.
- Be 6 years or older.
- If under 18, prove a welfare arrangement is in place.
- If applying while already in Australia, hold an eligible substantive visa.

**Notes**: A CoE is no longer valid if cancelled or the course is complete. A substantive visa ceases to be in effect if another substantive visa is granted.

**How long you can stay**: "This is a temporary visa to study up to 6 years. The type of course and its length will determine the length of stay." Primary school children starting enrolment in years 1–4 will generally only be granted a Student visa for a maximum of 3 years. To continue studying beyond the visa's validity, a new Student visa application is required (there is no extension mechanism for this visa itself).

**Genuine Student requirement**: Applicants must meet a "Genuine Student requirement" confirming that studying in Australia is the primary reason for the visa (detailed criteria on a linked sub-page not independently fetched in this session).

**Character and health requirements**: Applicant and any family members aged 16+ must meet the Department's character requirement; applicant and family members applying must meet the health requirement.

**Work rights while studying**: Student visa holders may generally work up to 48 hours a fortnight while their course is in session (no limit for students studying a research master's or doctoral degree, and their families).

**Financial requirements** (evidence of funds, stated on the page):
- **Annual income alternative**: evidence that the applicant's parents or partner had a personal annual income of at least **AUD 87,856** in the 12 months before applying; if family members are included, that threshold rises to **AUD 102,500**.
- **12-month living costs** (if relying on the standard funds calculation rather than the income alternative): students — **AUD 29,710**; partner accompanying — **AUD 10,394**; each child accompanying — **AUD 4,449**.
- **Course fees**: calculated as the course cost pro-rated to 12 months (the page gives worked examples, e.g., a 3-year AUD 50,000 course costs AUD 16,666 for the first 12 months).
- **Schooling costs**: at least **AUD 13,502** per year for each school-age child included in the application.
- **Travel costs**: AUD 2,500 (East/Southern Africa), AUD 3,000 (West Africa), AUD 2,000 (applying from anywhere else outside Australia), or AUD 1,000 (applying within Australia; AUD 1,500 if returning to Africa).

**English language and packaged courses**: Confirmation of Enrolment for each course in a packaged-course application is required; the last course in the package is treated as the main course of study, used to determine financial and English-language-skills evidence requirements. Course gaps in a package must generally be less than two calendar months (an exception applies for gaps that align with the standard academic-year break, e.g., November to February).

**Family inclusion**: Family members who may be included are the applicant's partner, or the applicant's/partner's dependent, unmarried child under 18. A child who turns 18 before the visa is finalized cannot be included and must apply separately.
