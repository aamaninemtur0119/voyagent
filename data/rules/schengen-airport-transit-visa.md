**CONFIRMED: Airport Transit Visa (ATV) nationality lists are NOT uniform across the Schengen area — they combine a common EU-wide list with additional lists set individually by each member state.**

**Common list**: Per the European Commission's Migration and Home Affairs page: "Some non-EU nationals must also hold an airport transit visa when connecting through the international transit areas of airports located in any of the Schengen States." This is the common baseline list, applicable uniformly across all Schengen states — it corresponds to Annex IV of the Visa Code (Regulation (EC) No 810/2009).

**Individual member-state lists (confirming the rule is NOT EU-wide-uniform)**: The same Commission page states: "For citizens of certain additional countries, an airport transit visa is required when connecting through the international transit areas of airports located in **some** of the Schengen countries" — i.e., beyond the common list, individual Schengen states impose their own additional airport transit visa requirements that do not apply across the whole area.

**Legal basis (EUR-Lex, Regulation (EC) No 810/2009, Article 3)**: Article 3(1) establishes the common list of nationalities (Annex IV) subject to the airport transit visa requirement across all Member States. Article 3(2) provides the individual member-state mechanism, stating (as fetched): "In urgent cases of mass influx of illegal immigrants, individual Member States may require nationals of third countries other than those referred to in paragraph 1 to hold an airport transit visa when passing through the international transit areas of airports situated on their territory." Member States must notify the European Commission of such nationally-imposed requirements. This is the legal mechanism confirming that ATV nationality lists are set per member state individually, layered on top of the common EU-wide list, rather than being a single uniform EU-wide list.

**Practical implication**: A traveler transiting through the international zone of a Schengen airport must check the specific requirements of the transit country (not just the common EU list), because a given nationality might not need an ATV to transit through one Schengen country's airport but might need one to transit through another's, depending on that country's additional national list.
