Source page: "Standard Visitor visa," GOV.UK, fetched directly (live page, no archive fallback needed).

**Who needs it**: Visa requirements depend on nationality. Some visitors have to apply for a Standard Visitor visa before travelling to the UK; others may instead qualify for an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA), or may enter visa-free depending on status. Applicants should check eligibility with the UK's official visa checker tool.

**Permitted activities**: tourism, visiting family, volunteer work (up to 30 days), business meetings, certain permitted paid engagements, short-term study courses (maximum 6 months), and medical treatment.

**Application process**: Eligible applicants apply for a Standard Visitor visa online before travelling to the UK. The earliest an application can be submitted is three months before the intended departure date.

**Typical stay length**: Standard Visitors can usually stay in the UK for up to 6 months. Extensions may be possible in specific circumstances, such as ongoing medical treatment.

**Fees**: £135 for stays up to 6 months, as stated on the page. A reduced fee of £74.50 applies if transiting is the applicant's only reason for coming to the UK.

**Eligibility requirements**: Applicants must demonstrate that they will leave the UK at the end of their visit, that they have sufficient funds to support themselves and cover return travel, and that they do not intend to make the UK their home through frequent or successive visits.
