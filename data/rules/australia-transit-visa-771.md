Source: "(Subclass 771) Transit visa," Australian Department of Home Affairs (immi.homeaffairs.gov.au), fetched live in this session (embedded JSON content block). Page meta-description: "This visa lets people be in Australia for up to 72 hours before they must leave Australia."

**What the visa is for** (exact page description): "This visa allows you up to 72 hours to transit Australia on your way to another country. You can also use this visa if you need to join a ship as a crew member and you hold a Maritime Crew Visa. If you already hold an Australian visa you may not need a Transit visa. Check VEVO or your ImmiAccount first. If we grant you a Transit visa this will end most other Australian visas that you may hold."

**All non-citizens need a visa to transit**: "All non-citizens need a visa or permission to enter Australia. This includes those staying for a short time or just transiting and not leaving the airport." A traveler who already holds another valid Australian temporary or permanent visa that permits transit does not need a separate Transit visa — but applying for and being granted a Transit visa will generally end that other visa (re-applying and paying any applicable fee would be required to use it again later).

**Eligibility ("You must")**:
- Be outside Australia (at time of application and grant).
- Be transiting Australia, with evidence of a confirmed booking to travel to another country within 72 hours of arriving in Australia, and hold the correct documents to enter that destination country.
- If joining the crew of an eligible ship: hold a Maritime Crew visa and the necessary crew documents, and sign on to the ship within 5 days of arrival.

**Duration**: "With this visa, you can transit through Australia for no longer than 72 hours." The visa cannot be extended — "You can't stay in Australia longer by extending this visa. If you want to stay longer, you must apply for another visa."

**Cost**: **"Free"** — the page's cost field states `visaCost: "Free"` directly (no application charge).

**Restrictions**:
- Cannot work in Australia.
- Cannot include family members on the same application — "You can't include family. You must make a separate application for each person travelling. This includes babies on their parent's passport."

**Processing time**: The page does not give a fixed number of days; it directs applicants to the Department's general "visa processing time guide tool" for an indication based on recently decided applications, describing it as "a guide only and not specific to your application."

**Application**: Apply online via the Department's ImmiAccount portal (the page links to `https://online.immi.gov.au/elp/app?action=new&formId=TV-AP-771`).

**Health cover note**: Applicants are personally liable for all healthcare costs while in Australia; the Department "might make adequate health cover for the whole of your stay a requirement for grant of this visa."
