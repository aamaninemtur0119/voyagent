Page title: "Exemption of Visa (Short-Term Stay)" — Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan. Page dated September 1, 2025 (last listed update on the official page).

Japan has reciprocal visa exemption arrangements with 74 countries and regions.

**Canada**: Listed among the 74 visa-exempt countries/regions (under "North America," alongside the United States), with no special note/condition attached. Standard 90-day stay applies.

**Brazil**: Listed among the 74 visa-exempt countries/regions (under "Latin America and the Caribbean"), but flagged with Note 2: visa exemption for Brazil applies only to holders of an ePassport in compliance with ICAO standards, in effect since September 30, 2023. Travelers without such an ePassport are required to obtain a visa in advance or will be refused entry.

**China**: NOT listed among the 74 visa-exempt countries/regions. No visa exemption arrangement exists for Chinese nationals under this page.

**India**: NOT listed among the 74 visa-exempt countries/regions. No visa exemption arrangement exists for Indian nationals under this page.

**Period of stay**: The period of stay granted upon landing permission is "15 days" for Indonesia and Thailand, "30 days" for Brunei and Qatar, and "90 days" for all other listed countries and regions (including Canada and Brazil).

**Notes/conditions relevant to specific countries (exact text from the page):**
- (Note 2) For nationals of Montenegro (since September 1, 2025), Peru (since July 1, 2025), Paraguay (since June 1, 2025), Panama (since April 1, 2024), Brazil (since September 30, 2023), United Arab Emirates (since November 1, 2022), Thailand (since July 1, 2013) and Serbia (since May 1, 2011): visas are not required only for holders of ePassport in compliance with ICAO standards. Those who do not hold such ePassport are requested to obtain a visa in advance, otherwise will be refused to enter Japan.
- (Note 8) For nationals of countries with visa exemptions permitting stays of up to 6 months under bilateral visa exemption arrangements (includes Austria, Germany, Ireland, Liechtenstein, Mexico, Switzerland, United Kingdom), those who wish to stay in Japan for more than 90 days are required to apply for an extension of the period of stay to the Ministry of Justice (Regional Immigration Bureau) before the permitted stay expires.

Full list of the 74 visa-exempt countries/regions as published on the page:
Asia: Brunei (30 days), Indonesia (Note 1) (15 days), Malaysia (Note 3), Republic of Korea, Singapore, Thailand (Note 2) (15 days), Hong Kong (Note 5), Macao (Note 6), Taiwan (Note 4).

Latin America and the Caribbean: Argentina, Bahamas, Barbados (Note 7), Brazil (Note 2), Chile, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Panama (Note 2), Paraguay (Note 2), Peru (Note 2), Mexico (Note 8), Suriname, Uruguay (Note 11).

Middle East: Israel, Qatar (Note 9), Türkiye (Note 7), United Arab Emirates (Note 2).
Africa: Lesotho (Note 7), Mauritius, Tunisia.
Europe: Andorra, Austria (Note 8), Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany (Note 8), Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland (Note 8), Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein (Note 8), Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro (Note 2), Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, San Marino, Serbia (Note 2), Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland (Note 8), United Kingdom (Note 8).
