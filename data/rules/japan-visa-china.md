Source page: "Visa information for Chinese nationals," Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan, page dated September 28, 2022 (as listed on the page itself).

Chinese passport holders are NOT on Japan's visa exemption list (see japan-visa-exemption-list.md) — a visa is required for tourism.

**Visa is required.** Tourist visits to Japan by Chinese nationals residing in China generally fall into these categories:

1. **Group Tourists (single-entry visa)**: As a general rule, tourist visits to Japan by Chinese nationals take the form of "group tours," based on Chinese regulations, with a period of stay of up to 15 days. Application must go through an approved Chinese travel agency, which handles the required documents. Participants must travel with a tour escort for the duration.

2. **Individual Tourists** (no escort required), with sub-types:
   - (A) Single-entry visa: issued to applicants with a certain level of financial capability (or their family members), or to students/recent graduates (within 3 years) of listed ordinary Chinese universities. Period of stay: 15 or 30 days. Applicant must apply through an approved Chinese travel agency.
   - (B) Multiple-entry visa for individual tourists visiting Okinawa or the Tohoku region (Aomori, Iwate, Miyagi, Akita, Yamagata, Fukushima): valid for 3 years, period of stay up to 30 days per visit, requires at least one overnight stay in the specified region and certain financial-capability requirements.
   - (C) Multiple-entry visa for individual tourists with sufficient financial capability (no destination requirement): valid for 3 years, period of stay up to 30 days per visit.
   - (D) Multiple-entry visa for visitors with substantially high income: valid for 5 years, period of stay up to 90 days per visit. Holders may not engage in paid business activities.

3. Chinese nationals residing outside China with substantially high income or sufficient financial capability may also obtain multiple-entry visas (5-year validity/90 days per visit for high income; 3-year validity/30 days per visit for sufficient financial capability) via the Japanese Embassy/Consulate-General covering their place of residence.

**Note**: Visits for short-term business affairs or visiting relatives/acquaintances require a different visa category ("short-term business affairs" or "visit to relatives/acquaintances" visa), not a tourist visa, except for the high-income multiple-entry visa category.

**eVISA availability for China (per the JAPAN eVISA system page, snapshot dated 2026-06-25, page itself dated May 15, 2026)**: China is listed under a separate eligibility category (not the general country list of Australia, Brazil, Cambodia, Canada, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Taiwan, UK, USA). For China specifically: "Nationals of China, who reside in China" are eligible to apply for the JAPAN eVISA, but application must be made "through an accredited agency" — not directly on an individual basis. The eVISA is a single-entry short-term stay visa for tourism, and for nationals of China residing in China, the electronic visa is issued with a period of stay of either "15 days" or "30 days" (not the standard 90 days given to most other eVISA-eligible nationalities). Only ordinary passport holders are eligible. Applicants may be required to appear in person for an interview at the Japanese overseas establishment with jurisdiction over their residence.

Documents required are not itemized on this page; the page directs applicants to consult an approved Chinese travel agency (for paper-visa group/individual tourist categories) or the accredited agency handling eVISA applications for China.
