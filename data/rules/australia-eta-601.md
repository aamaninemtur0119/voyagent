Source: "Subclass 601 Electronic Travel Authority," Australian Department of Home Affairs (immi.homeaffairs.gov.au), fetched live in this session (embedded JSON content block) and cross-checked against a 2026-07-31 Wayback Machine snapshot of the same page.

**CONFIRMED: Canada is on the ETA (subclass 601) eligible passport list.**

Exact eligibility text from the page: "You must hold a valid passport from one of these countries or jurisdictions to be eligible for an ETA. You cannot apply for an ETA with a non-citizen passport, certificate of identity or other travel document, such as a Titre de Voyage."

Full eligible passport/jurisdiction list as published on the page:
Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Brunei, **Canada**, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hong Kong (SAR of China), Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malaysia, Malta, Monaco, Norway, Portugal, Republic of San Marino, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Taiwan (excluding official or diplomatic passports), The Netherlands, United Kingdom—British Citizen, United Kingdom—British National (Overseas), United States of America, Vatican City.

**Other eligibility notes**: Applicants holding more than one passport must declare this in the ETA application and provide details of other passports. An ETA-eligible passport holder who is also an Australian citizen cannot apply for an ETA (Australian citizens have automatic right of entry and do not need a visa).

**Visa terms**:
- **Purpose/permitted activities**: visit family or friends, cruise or holiday, undertake some business visitor activities.
- **Stay duration**: Up to 3 months at a time per entry.
- **Validity**: A temporary visa valid for travel to Australia for either 12 months, or for the period the passport is valid, whichever is shorter. Granted with multiple entry — travelers may leave and re-enter Australia as many times as they want while it is valid, remaining up to 3 months after each entry. The 12-month visa period starts on the date it is granted (unless the holder already holds another substantive visa in effect).
- **Cost**: A service charge of **AUD $20** applies to use the Australian ETA app; no other charge is payable for an ETA.
- **Processing**: In most cases, the result of an ETA application is advised immediately; it may take longer if the application is incomplete or further information is required. Applicants are advised not to make travel arrangements until the ETA is granted.
- **Work restriction**: ETA holders cannot undertake paid work for an Australian employer; doing so may result in cancellation of the ETA.
- **Extended/frequent stays**: Travelers who stay for long periods or make frequent visits may be asked about their travel purpose on subsequent entry; if not found to be a genuine visitor, the ETA may be cancelled. Those wanting to stay longer than 3 months at a time need to apply for a different visa.
