Fetched directly (live official page, no archive fallback needed).

**Standard fee: EUR 90 for adults.**

**Reduced fee: EUR 45 for children aged 6-12.** Children under 6 are exempt (no fee).

**Nationality-specific reduced fees** stated on the same page: Armenia, Azerbaijan, and Belarus nationals pay EUR 35; Cabo Verde nationals pay EUR 67.50. Neither China nor India appears on this reduced-fee list, so the standard EUR 90 adult fee applies to both.

No effective date is stated on the fetched page — these are presented as the current standard fee, uniform across all Schengen member states (fee amount is set at EU level via the Visa Code, not by individual member states).
