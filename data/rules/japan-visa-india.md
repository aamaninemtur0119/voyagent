Source page: "The JAPAN eVISA system (electronic visa)," Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan, page dated May 15, 2026 (as listed on the page itself: "As of May 15 2026, the JAPAN eVISA system is available for nationals/people residing in the following countries/regions").

**Visa is required** for Indian nationals — India is NOT on Japan's visa exemption list (see japan-visa-exemption-list.md).

**eVISA option DOES currently exist for India.** India is listed in eligibility group (4) along with Hong Kong, Indonesia, Macau, Mongolia, the Republic of Korea (except jurisdiction of the Consulate General of Japan in Jeju), Singapore, and United Arab Emirates: "All foreign nationals/people residing in [this group], except those who are exempt from short-term visa" may apply for an eVISA. Unlike China/Philippines/Vietnam, this group is not restricted to accredited-agency-only applications — the page groups India with the countries where application is made "through the JAPAN eVISA website" directly (the accredited-agency-required rule is stated separately for China, the Philippines, and Vietnam only).

**Visa type**: Single-entry short-term stay visa for the purpose of Tourism, valid for a period of stay of up to 90 days (the standard duration; India is not listed among the exceptions — China, Vietnam, Philippines — that receive shorter 15/30-day eVISAs).

**Eligibility conditions**:
- Only ordinary passport holders are eligible to apply through the JAPAN eVISA website.
- Applicable only to travel to Japan by air or sea (sea limited to international scheduled passenger ferries between Japan and Busan or Shanghai).
- During the application process, the applicant may be requested to appear in person at the Japanese overseas establishment with jurisdiction over their place of residence for an interview.
- Travelers with an eVISA are required to display the "Visa issuance notice" at the airport via an internet connection; a PDF, screenshot, or printed copy is not accepted.

**If not applying via eVISA**, or for other purposes/long-term stay, applicants submit to the Japanese Embassy, Consulate-General, or Consular office with jurisdiction over their place of residence, resulting in a visa sticker in the passport rather than an electronic visa.
